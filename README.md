## Background

This project began with a fairly mundane problem: trying to identify old digital files accumulated across NAS storage and cloud drives.

Files can survive for decades while their context does not. A filename may tell us very little about what an artifact is, where it came from, which version it represents, what it depends on, what rights apply to it, or whether it can still be experienced.

The problem becomes more serious as digital services and software platforms disappear. An artifact may remain in someone's possession while the software, servers, authentication systems, distribution platforms, or other infrastructure required to use it are shut down.

Digital preservation therefore is not simply a matter of keeping the bytes. We also need to preserve enough context to understand what those bytes are, where they came from, what may legally be done with them, and what is required to experience them.

DAC is an attempt to explore that problem through an open, portable specification.
